01 — About

Crafting Spaces
That Endure

Raymond Aghakhani is a licensed general contractor with over three decades of experience building some of Southern California's most distinguished residential and commercial properties. Since establishing his practice in 1991, Raymond has become a trusted name among Los Angeles and Orange County's most discerning homeowners, developers, and investors.

His work spans the full spectrum of high-end construction — from bespoke custom residences crafted to the finest detail, to the complex project management of multi-unit commercial buildings and condominium complexes. Every project is approached with the same unwavering commitment: excellence, integrity, and an uncompromising eye for quality.
