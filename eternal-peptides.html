Our Process

Science

We don't cut corners. Every batch goes through a multi-stage quality assurance pipeline before it ever reaches a researcher's hands.

01

Solid-Phase Peptide Synthesis

All peptides are synthesized using Fmoc SPPS methodology in our ISO-certified facility, ensuring sequence accuracy down to the last amino acid.

02

HPLC Purification

Each batch undergoes high-performance liquid chromatography purification to remove truncated sequences and synthesis byproducts.

03

Mass Spectrometry Verification

Independent mass spec analysis confirms molecular weight and sequence identity before any batch is approved for release.

04

Lyophilization & Cold Storage

Peptides are freeze-dried to remove all solvent, sealed under inert gas, and cold-stored to preserve bioactivity through shipping.

Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Are these peptides for human use?+
All peptides sold by Eternal Peptides are for research purposes only. They are not intended for human or animal use, consumption, or clinical application. We sell exclusively to licensed researchers and research institutions.
How do I verify my batch purity?+
Every vial ships with a QR code printed on the label. Scan it to access your batch-specific Certificate of Analysis, including the HPLC chromatogram, mass spectrum, and purity percentage from our lab.
How should peptides be stored?+
Lyophilized (dry) peptides are stable at room temperature for up to 12 months if kept away from light and moisture. For long-term storage, we recommend −20°C. Once reconstituted, store at 4°C and use within 30 days.
What solvent should I use for reconstitution?+
Most peptides reconstitute well in bacteriostatic water. For hydrophobic sequences, 0.1% acetic acid or dilute DMSO may be required. Each product page includes specific reconstitution guidance, and our team is available to assist.
How long does shipping take?+
In-stock items ship within 48 hours. Domestic US orders typically arrive in 2–4 business days. International shipping is 5–10 business days. All orders are shipped with tracking and discreet packaging.
Do you offer bulk / research institution pricing?+
Yes. We work directly with universities, biotech companies, and research institutions on custom bulk orders. Contact us at research@eternalpeptides.com with your institution details and compound requirements for a custom quote.
